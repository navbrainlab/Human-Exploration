Gaze-shift speed is shown in Supplementary Fig. S6i.

Boxes show medians and interquartile ranges; whiskers extend to observations within 1.5 interquartile ranges; diamonds denote means. Tests are paired Wilcoxon signed-rank, one-sided in b and two-sided in c–e; Holm correction is applied in b and false-discovery-rate correction in d and e. All values, intervals, sample inclusion and significance annotations are retained from the original gaze figure. *P < 0.05, **P < 0.01, ***P < 0.001.
