# Fig. 1 | Task Structure and Learning in Multidimensional Environments

**a,** Task 1 meal arrangement and prior-practice design. Participants arranged
nine meal bundles into three ordered rows and received row-weighted feedback.
The experienced group completed 3D-NE (up to 30 trials) followed by 4D-E (up to
50 trials; 51 participants); the comparison group completed 4D-NE without
prior practice (up to 50 trials; 54 participants).

**b,** Task 2 room choice and prior-observation design. Participants chose four
room scenes from 16 options and received feedback. Observation (Obs) and
no-observation (NObs) groups each contained 40 participants and completed up
to 60 trials. Dashed arrows indicate omission of the initial practice or
observation stage, not waiting periods. Both tasks conclude with survey and
interview. Stimuli and furniture components originate from the supplied PPTX.
Feedback values 78 and 48 and the illustrated feature values are schematic
examples, not analysed trials. Neutral gray frames and connectors delineate
the feature spaces in both tasks. Orange-red local selection frames do not
encode reward magnitude; the feature value scale is separate.

**c,e,** Task 1 and Task 2 behavioral results, respectively; labels
retain the user's existing c/e convention. Left: mean score by normalized trial, after
centred five-trial within-participant smoothing; bands show +/-1 s.e.m.
across participants. Normalized trial is (trial − 1)/(T − 1), with T = 30 for 3D-NE, 50 for 4D-E and 4D-NE, and 60 for Obs and NObs; the first and final scheduled trials map to 0 and 1. The same transformation is applied to the mean curves and uncertainty bands, without rebinning. Right-hand personal-best endpoints remain in absolute trials. Missing trials following observed completion are filled
with score 100. Right: the first trial attaining each participant's personal
maximum, including non-completers (Task 1, n=51/51/54; Task 2, n=40/40).
Both right-hand subplots label this endpoint `Trial of best score`.
This endpoint is not the time to a common performance criterion or to score
100: earlier personal-best trials can also indicate an earlier plateau.
Task 2 uses the observed noisy feedback score (`score_noisy`).
Boxes show medians and interquartile ranges; whiskers extend to observations
within 1.5 interquartile ranges. Individual and outlier symbols are hidden,
not excluded. Original two-sided, unadjusted Mann-Whitney U comparisons are
retained: 4D-E vs 4D-NE, P=0.0207; Obs vs NObs,
P=0.0453 (U=591.5). Full-precision P values remain in the frozen source
records. *P<0.05. Double slashes indicate omitted zero-to-lower
axis intervals. Full-score rates remain in Supplementary Fig. S1.

Main axis lines begin/end at their outer displayed major ticks. Task 2’s right-hand category display is translated left at unchanged span and data coordinates to reduce the ylabel-to-box distance; all boxes, whiskers, category labels and comparison annotations retain their definitions. Source uncertainty bounds, sample inclusion and P values are unchanged. The original detailed Methods are preserved in `source/original_results_caption.md`.
