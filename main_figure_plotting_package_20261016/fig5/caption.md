**Fig. 5 | Dimension awareness, exploration and prior observation.** **a,** Percentage of DIS trials by reported dimension-identification level in Task 1 (None, Partial, Perfect; n = 7, 15, 82). **b,** Frequency of dimension-related terms in DIS-context reports (DIS) and the full report (Others), paired within participants (72 pairs per task). The Others comparator retains the inclusive full report, including DIS-context material; it is not a non-DIS-only subset. **c–e,** Dimensions fixated before the task (c), initially reported noticed dimensions (d) and distinct task dimensions explored through DIS in trials 1–25 (e), comparing prior-observation (Obs) and no-observation (NObs) groups. **f,** Self-reported reward-relevant dimensions at early and final stages (mean ± s.e.m.); legend suffixes summarize the Early–Final comparison within each group (Obs **, NObs ns). No significance brackets or annotations are drawn inside f.

Boxes show medians and interquartile ranges; whiskers extend to observations within 1.5 interquartile ranges, and diamonds indicate means. Individual symbols are hidden in a and c–e without excluding observations. In b, faint lines connect participants and dark lines connect group means. Sample sizes for c–f are 37/39, 40/40, 40/40 and 39/40 (Obs/NObs). In a, the original two-sided Mann–Whitney U tests apply within-group 5th–95th percentile trimming only to the tested samples (Task 1: 5/13/73); displayed unadjusted P values are 0.02034 and 0.01511 (adjacent levels). All original observations remain in the boxes. In b, paired two-sided Wilcoxon tests give unadjusted P = 2.43 × 10⁻⁶ and 2.27 × 10⁻⁴ for Tasks 1 and 2. In c–e, original two-sided Mann–Whitney tests and correction families are retained (Benjamini–Hochberg in c). In f, exploratory two-sided paired t-tests within groups are Holm-adjusted: Obs, P = 0.00665; NObs, P = 0.195. The separate exploratory between-group change contrast is a two-sided Welch t-test (unadjusted P = 0.00192), reported here and not encoded by the within-group legend suffixes. Exact source statistics are retained in the source packages. *P < 0.05, **P < 0.01, ***P < 0.001; ns, P ≥ 0.05. Double slashes indicate omitted zero-to-lower-bound intervals. DIS-frequency axes show source proportions × 100. Icons identify survey, eye-tracking or task-behaviour evidence.
