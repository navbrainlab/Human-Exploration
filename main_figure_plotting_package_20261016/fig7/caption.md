The relation illustrates the deterministic limit with previous-attention inertia, uniform lapse and dimension shocks omitted and the priority scale set to one; it is not the complete fitted update or its expected weight. Normalization makes the displayed proportionality an equality. Feature and residual item shocks are also omitted graphically. Bars are illustrative.
