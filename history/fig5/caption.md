**Fig. 5 | Dimension-selective gaze and exploration following observation.** **a,** Trial-count-matched fixation-density maps for plant-dimension DIS and other trials (246 per condition); colour indicates relative display intensity. **b,** Dwell-time allocation across fixated dimensions (rows) and dimensions tested through DIS (columns). Outlined diagonal cells identify the tested dimension. Within each participant, allocation to this dimension was compared with mean allocation to the remaining dimensions. **c–f,** Gaze coverage area (c), mean dwell time per fixated image (d), gaze-shift speed (e) and number of task dimensions fixated (f), comparing DIS with other trials. **g–i,** Dimensions fixated before the task (g), initial reports of noticed dimensions (h) and distinct task dimensions explored through DIS during trials 1–25 (i), comparing Obs and NObs. **j,** Self-reported score-relevant dimension counts at early and final stages (mean ± s.e.m.; descriptive).

Icons denote eye tracking (a–g), survey responses (h, j) and task behaviour (i). Boxes show medians and interquartile ranges; whiskers extend to observations within 1.5 interquartile ranges; diamonds denote means. In c–f, faint lines connect participant pairs and dark lines connect means. Open circles in g–i denote outliers. Sample sizes are 71, 66, 52, 58 and 62 participants in b (Sofa, Table, Carpet, Plant and Painting, respectively); 75 participant pairs in c–f; and 37/39, 40/40, 40/40 and 39/40 Obs/NObs participants in g–j. Tests are Wilcoxon signed-rank in b–f (one-sided in b; otherwise two-sided) and two-sided Mann–Whitney U in g–i. Source-defined adjustments are Holm (b), false discovery rate (d, f) and Benjamini–Hochberg (g). Exact statistics are provided in Methods and Source Data. \*P < 0.05, \*\*P < 0.01, \*\*\*P < 0.001. DIS, dimension-invariant search; others, other trials; Obs/NObs, with/without prior observation; s.e.m., standard error of the mean.
