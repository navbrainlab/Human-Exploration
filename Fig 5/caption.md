**Fig. 5 | Dimension-selective gaze during DIS and broader early exploration following observation.** **a,** Trial-count-matched fixation-density maps for plant-dimension DIS and others trials (246 each); colour indicates relative display intensity. **b,** Gaze dwell-time proportions across tested DIS dimensions and fixated dimensions; a blue–red scale encodes nonnegative proportions, not signed effects. Outlined diagonal cells denote matched dimensions; tests compare each participant’s matched-dimension allocation with their mean allocation to the remaining dimensions. **c–f,** Gaze coverage area (c), mean dwell time per fixated image (d), gaze-shift speed (e) and number of task dimensions fixated (f), comparing DIS and others. **g–i,** Dimensions fixated before the task (g), dimensions initially reported as noticed (h) and distinct task dimensions explored through DIS during trials 1–25 (i), comparing Obs and NObs participants. **j,** Self-reported score-relevant dimensions at the early and final stages (mean ± s.e.m.; descriptive). The displayed trajectories do not establish a group-by-stage interaction or recognition accuracy.

Icons identify eye tracking (a–g), survey responses (h, j) and task behaviour (i). Boxes show medians and interquartile ranges, whiskers reach observations within 1.5 interquartile ranges, and diamonds show means. In c–f, faint lines connect paired participant observations; dark lines connect means. Open circles in g–i denote outliers. Samples comprise 75 participant pairs in c–f; 71/66/52/58/62 participants for the five dimensional comparisons in b; and 37/39, 40/40, 40/40 and 39/40 Obs/NObs participants in g–j. Tests are paired Wilcoxon signed-rank in b–f (one-sided in b, otherwise two-sided) and two-sided Mann–Whitney U in g–i. Adjustments are Holm in b, false discovery rate in d and f, and Benjamini–Hochberg in g. Panel j has no interaction test. Exact P values and source-defined corrections are retained in the companion statistics. *P < 0.05, **P < 0.01, ***P < 0.001. DIS, dimension-invariant search; Obs/NObs, with/without prior observation; s.e.m., standard error of the mean.
