Reproduce with code/render_fig5_evidence_layout.py from the project root. Existing cached inputs are used; no experiments, statistics, fitting or simulation are rerun beyond the primary renderer's established summary calculations.

Panel mapping from the primary edition: a→a, c→b, b→c, d→d, e→e, f→f, h→g, i→h, g→i, j→j. Panel b is a 37 × 30.6 mm rectangular heatmap, following the user-provided size reference; a and b share the same top edge. Only matrix-cell display geometry changes; the data and blue–red normalization remain unchanged. Panels c–j retain 33.3 mm plot-area height. Acquisition-modality icons match the primary edition, with their evidence-to-panel mapping updated. Box fill opacity and paired-point/line opacity match the primary figure. Display labels remain DIS/others and Obs/NObs.

The source data and scientific definitions are those documented in ../fig5/methods.md, interpreted with the above panel mapping. Gaze maps retain the existing display palette conversion and qualitative Low/High scale; they are not new quantitative density estimates. The selectivity heatmap uses the blue–red RdBu_r palette and retains the 0–0.3 normalization and Holm-adjusted within-participant comparisons of matched-dimension allocation versus mean allocation to the remaining dimensions. Its colour scale encodes proportion, not signed effect. This comparison alone does not establish control for image area, spatial position or visual salience. Paired participant points and all group observations remain included. Boxplots show medians/IQR and 1.5-IQR whiskers; diamonds denote means. The stage trajectory retains mean ± SEM and is descriptive. Questionnaire counts can exceed the number of task dimensions and must not be interpreted as recognition accuracy. Exploration in panel i counts distinct dimensions explored through DIS during trials 1–25, not trials to explore all dimensions.
